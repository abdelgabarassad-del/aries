Hi my love,

Four years ago I met you, and I had no idea what was about to happen to me. We were not even together yet, and somehow you were already the person I wanted to tell everything to.

Loving you from far away is not easy. I would trade every screen in the world for one real hug, and every good night call for sitting next to you. But you make the distance feel small. You show up, you laugh, you pout at me through the camera, and suddenly I am home.

Thank you for being soft when I need it and silly when I need that more. Thank you for your patience, your style, your heart, and for every little moment you share with me. You make my world better just by being in it.

I can't wait for the day I don't have to say goodbye at the end of a call. Until then, I am yours, every day, across every mile.

Happy anniversary. I love you, always. ♥
